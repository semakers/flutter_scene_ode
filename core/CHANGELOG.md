## 0.1.0

First public release, as `flutter_scene_ode_core`.

- Renamed from the core of `nairda_ode` 0.5.1, the physics backend of the Nairda
  educational robotics app. Same code.
- The Flutter plugin that bundles the binaries is `flutter_scene_ode`.
- Works with `package:scene` 0.2 and 0.3 (the physics contract did not change).
