## 0.1.0

First public release, as `flutter_scene_ode`.

- Renamed from `nairda_ode` 0.5.1, the physics backend of the Nairda
  educational robotics app. Same code and the same native binaries.
- The pure-Dart core is published separately as `flutter_scene_ode_core`.
- Works with `package:scene` 0.2 and 0.3 (the physics contract did not change).
- Licensed BSD 3-Clause. ODE is redistributed under its BSD-style license.
- The history before the rename, in Spanish, is in
  [doc/HISTORIA_nairda_ode.md](doc/HISTORIA_nairda_ode.md).
